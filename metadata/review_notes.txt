The only permission usage-description string in the build settings is for Camera: "This app does not use the camera." The app does not request camera access, so that prompt does not appear. On first launch the system may ask to allow notifications. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Amerce is a parlor wheel for hosts who deal dares to a room. Each Spin pins the next unused dare from one locked pack onto a landed name as an open forfeit until that name taps Clear. It is meant for a live party on one device. No credentials or demo account are needed. Cold launch shows Pin a dare, then Clear when done, then Stack the night. Tap Continue on the first two pages and Start on the last, or tap Skip on any page. The home screen titled Amerce then shows the heading Forfeit and the wheel. If you see The wheel is empty, tap Add names to open Settings, type a Name plate, tap Seat for one or more names, then under Pack tap Lock on Wax parlor slips, Lamp room, or Glove table. Close Settings. If names are already seated and a pack is locked, tap Spin from home. The status line reads Spin pins the next unused dare on a name. Tap Spin. After the wheel lands, tap Clear on that name when the dare is done. Open History to read Lands by name and each night. Open Settings for Names, Pack, Haptics, Re-run onboarding, Contact Amerce, and Reset all data. The strip that shows Unused dares, or Lock pack, opens Locked pack. Names, packs, forfeits, and History stay on this device, and Contact Amerce opens the support page. Behaviour does not differ by region. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies, because Amerce is a party dare wheel and does not take stakes, give medical advice, or run a licensed game of chance. The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations.
