Three behaviours are deliberate and can look like defects on a quick pass. Please check these first.

1. While the wheel is turning, "Spin" and "Clear" stay disabled and the status reads "The wheel is turning." Wait for the land; both controls return once the forfeit is pinned.
2. When unused dares run out, "Spin" will not pin. Status shows "The pack is spent. Lock a new pack to spin." Tap "Lock pack" on the arena, or open "Locked pack" / Settings and tap "Lock" on "Wax parlor slips", "Lamp room", or "Glove table".
3. "Re-run onboarding" in Settings closes Settings and opens the three-page pamphlet again on purpose. Finish with "Start" or "Skip" to return to the parlor.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch may briefly show a blank window, then the native parlor. The system may present the standard notifications alert. If onboarding is unfinished, the pamphlet opens: "Pin a dare", "Clear when done", then "Stack the night". Tap "Continue" on the first two pages and "Start" on the last, or "Skip" on any page.
- The main arena titled "Amerce" opens with hero "Forfeit". If "The wheel is empty." appears, tap "Add names". In Settings, enter a "Name plate", tap "Seat", then under "Pack" tap "Lock" on a pack. Close Settings.
- With names seated and a pack locked, tap "Spin". After the land, the forfeit rail and hub show the dare; tap "Clear" when it is done.
- Tap "History" for "Lands by name" and the night run, or the empty copy "The night has no forfeits yet." with "Back to the wheel".
- Tap "Settings" for "Names", "Pack", "Haptics" ("Landing climax on" / "Landing climax off"), "Re-run onboarding", "Contact Amerce", and "Reset all data". "Unused dares" or "Lock pack" opens "Locked pack".

Finishing onboarding with no pack locked seats "Wax parlor slips". Physical devices start with no sample name plates. Unfinished parlor state — seated names, locked pack and remaining dares, open forfeits, haptics preference, onboarding flag, and nights — resumes across launches.
